# Licensing and provenance

Since 2026-11-16 this repository is public and has a top-level Apache-2.0 `LICENSE` file (commit 7bb6020, owner decision). Engine-source and research-package rights are not uniform, and some copied or adapted methods have unresolved ownership or redistribution terms. The [release record](rs-release-gate.md) keeps those gaps open; section 10 of the record states that the public step closes none of them.

Before publishing, redistributing, or reusing any method outside its approved local evaluation, check the [engine source ledger](engine-source-ledger.md) and the relevant engine guide. Record the source, version, modifications, dependencies, and permitted use. Do not infer permission from a package being available locally or from its implementation being callable.
The source origin, author attribution status and go / no-go decision for public distribution of the unusual-pairs engine (catalog name "Find Unusual Pairs Across a Research Collection", formerly called RS) are recorded in the [release-gate record](rs-release-gate.md).

Third-party dependencies keep their own licenses; see the package metadata and upstream notices. This document is a project status note, not a license grant or legal opinion.
