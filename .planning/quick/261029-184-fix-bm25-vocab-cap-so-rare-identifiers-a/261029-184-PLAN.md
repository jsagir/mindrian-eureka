<objective>
Fix the BM25 vocabulary cap so a rare identifier shared by 2 documents is kept, not dropped by its spelling.

Defect (PROVEN by the probe at /tmp/claude-1000/-home-jsagi/6524eaaa-94db-4c53-a8de-9f6d15b98f81/scratchpad/rare_term_test3.py): `_bm25_cosine` ranks the vocabulary by document frequency descending, then by term ascending, and cuts at `max_features=5000`. When the corpus has more than 5000 distinct terms, the cut lands inside the rare band. A code such as `ZZ-ZYXW` shared by 2 documents dies by its alphabet position (lift 0.000). `AA-0001` survives (lift 0.009). The same ordering exists in `hsi_reference._term_count_matrix` (used by `compute_bm25_similarity` with cap 100000 and by `compute_lsa_similarity` with cap 500).

Purpose: a rare shared identifier is the strongest lexical link between 2 documents. The cap must drop terms that cannot link documents (df == 1) first, then the most common terms, never the rarest.
Output: 1 regression test file, 2 source edits, 1 summary, 1 state row, 1 commit. No push.
</objective>
