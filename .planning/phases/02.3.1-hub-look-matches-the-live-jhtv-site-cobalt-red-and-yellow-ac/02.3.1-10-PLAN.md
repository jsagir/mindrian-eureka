<objective>
Close three gaps that the orchestrator found in the plan 04 renders, before the owner looks at the hub in plan 09.

G1. The card variant has no text, so every card is the same picture with different bar lengths. Under the owner's words of 2026-11-08 ("the images need to represent the core of the opportunity not be a generic image", D-07, D-09) that is a generic image. The card must carry the core: the top 3 distinctive words of each record as small mono labels under its marker, and the shared words (when any) in a chip on the dashed link. The 7 fan lines have no readable meaning; they are removed. Their bytes pay for the words, so CARD_MAX_BYTES stays 4096 and the 5054 hard ceiling holds.

G2. Distinctive words show as stems (claus for clauses, program for programs). The owner reads a cut word as a defect. Display the most frequent surface form of each stem from the record's own text. Stemming stays for matching, never for display. This applies to the card, the detail and the terms payload that the bank rows (plan 06), the panel (plan 07) and the HTML twin (plan 08) render.

G3. hub-data.js grew about 8 times on the public fixture (393655 to 3122578 bytes) and the 3000-record run is 9.9 MB, because every pair key carries both inline SVGs. Decision of this plan: the figures stay inline, with a measured budget and a load-time probe. Reasons (all PROVEN by reading the code and the tests): (1) the hub opens from file:// and its CSP says connect-src 'none', so the browser cannot fetch a figure file; (2) an img element cannot follow the data-theme toggle, and plan 05 tests that figure shapes follow the theme through the class rules of hub.css; (3) a locked hub must hold no svg text, no word and no title in clear (test_locked_build_holds_no_word_and_no_figure_in_clear), so plain figure files next to hub.html would leak the words of a locked hub; (4) plans 03, 05, 06, 07 and 08 all read HubApp.data.figures.pairs[key] synchronously through HubApp.figure, two of them in this same wave. A separate store needs script shards with per-shard envelopes and asynchronous consumers; that is a follow-up plan after plan 08, opened only if the measurement of this plan fails its budget.

Purpose: the cards become leads a person can tell apart and read; no word on the page looks cut; the size of the data file has a recorded budget and a measured load time.
Output: terms.py with display forms, figures.py with word-bearing cards, figure_copy.py, hub.css text classes, re-recorded goldens (six SVG, two JSON), the rules files, a load-timing probe, and two PNG renders outside the repo for the owner.
</objective>
