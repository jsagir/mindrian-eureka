# Phase 02.3.1 Plan 02: Distinctive words of a run Summary

**A standard-library tf-idf word counter (`hub/terms.py`) that gives every record its 5 most distinctive words and every pair the words both records share, byte-stable across runs, with golden lists of the public fixture and the rule written in `docs/brand/hub-terms-rules.md`.**

## Accomplishments

- `build_terms(records, pairs)` returns `{"records": {id: top 5}, "pairs": {pair_id: shared, at most 6}}`. Formula `score = tf * log((N + 1) / (df + 0.5))`, natural log, scores rounded to 9 decimals, ties by the word, depth list of 30, shared words ordered by the larger rank of the two.
- 13 tests pin the rule. RED commit first (all 13 failed: 12 on the missing module, 1 on the missing rules file), then the GREEN commit (13 passed).
- Golden lists of the public fixture: 36 record lists and 225 pair lists (200 wording pairs plus 63 unusual pairs, 38 shared ids). 157 of the 225 pairs share no word in their depth lists; 68 share 1 to 6 words.
- The rules file states that the words are a screening aid and not evidence, lists the 160-word stop list, the 9-decimal reason, the limits, the privacy note and the book status "book silent, proposed segment: distinctive words".

## Decisions Made

- Where the behavior block of Task 1 and the written rule (item 3) disagreed, the written rule won, because the plan names it "the contract of this plan". Two examples differ from the behavior block: `boxes` gives `boxe` (the es fold needs a length above 5, and `boxes` has 5 letters) and `bias` stays `bias` (the s fold needs a length above 4). Both are pinned in the test as recorded limits; `indexes` gives `index` pins the xes fold and `canvas` gives `canva` pins the s-fold limit.
- The ses fold of the written rule cuts `clauses` to `claus` and `phases` to `phas` on the fixture. The plan says: do not change the rule for a defect of this kind; record it as a limit. Recorded in the rules file under Limits. The orchestrator should know that this is the one visible regression against the prototype (which gave `clause`).
- `build_terms` sorts the unique `(pair_id, left, right)` triples and keeps the first entry of a pair id. The two engines give the same two record ids for one pair id, so the choice is stable.
