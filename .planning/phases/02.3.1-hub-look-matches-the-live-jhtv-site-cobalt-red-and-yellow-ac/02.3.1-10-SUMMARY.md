## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Plan example contradicts the fold] `clauses` against `clause` are two stems**
- **Found during:** Task 1 (test design)
- **Issue:** The behavior block expects `clauses` to display from a counter `{clauses: 2, clause: 1}` and a pair to share `clause` (3 against 2). Under the written fold `clauses` gives `claus` but `clause` keeps its `e` and stays `clause`; the two never meet in one counter, so the plan's example cannot hold.
- **Fix:** As the plan allows ("else pick a constructed pair and state it in the test"): the record test keeps the plan's text and checks that the stem `claus` displays as `clauses` and that `clause` is its own stem; the shared test uses `programs` against `program` (both fold to `program`); the alphabetical tie uses a constructed counter (`analyze` against `analyse`), because no two real tokens of equal length fold to one stem (every fold shortens by a fixed amount).
- **Files:** tests/test_hub_terms.py. **Commits:** 060c43a, ad3c193.

**2. [Rule 1 - Plan arithmetic] CLASS_TABLE holds 25 classes, not 24**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (RED)
- **Issue:** The plan says "24 classes: 14 shapes, 6 text sizes, 5 text modifiers"; 14 + 6 + 5 is 25 (plan 04 had 15 + 4 + 5 = 24; this plan removes 1 and adds 2).
- **Fix:** The test asserts 25 (`CLASS_COUNT`); the rules file says 25.
- **Files:** tests/test_hub_figures.py, docs/brand/hub-figure-rules.md. **Commits:** 6a74d91, 539c479.

**3. [Rule 1 - Wrong copy] The card legend said the two squares are "at the left"**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (copy)
- **Issue:** The plan's edit of `G18.legend.card` only drops the decoration sentence and adds the words sentence; the sentence "The two squares at the left stand for the two records" would then be false, because marker B is now at the right.
- **Fix:** "The two squares at the top, record A at the left and record B at the right, stand for the two records and the dashed line between them is the proposed pair." The rest of the legend is as the plan says.
- **Files:** src/mindrian_eureka/hub/figure_copy.py. **Commit:** 539c479.

**4. [Rule 2 - Determinism] Integer label width instead of a float ceiling**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (GREEN)
- **Issue:** `ceil(len * size * 0.66)` can differ from `ceil(len * 0.6 * size * 1.1)` by one unit when the float product lands a hair above an integer, and the test and the module would then disagree on the x of a record B label.
- **Fix:** `label_width = -(-len * size * 66 // 100)` in the module (`LABEL_ADVANCE_PERCENT` 66) and the same form in the test. Same values as the plan's rule, exact on every machine.
- **Files:** src/mindrian_eureka/hub/figures.py, tests/test_hub_figures.py. **Commit:** 539c479.

**5. [Rule 1 - Measured number] Public fixture hub-data.js size**
- **Found during:** Task 3 (docs)
- **Issue:** A first draft of the Storage section carried an unmeasured size for the public fixture.
- **Fix:** Replaced by the measured 3106070 bytes before the commit.
- **Files:** docs/brand/hub-figure-rules.md. **Commit:** 879258f.
