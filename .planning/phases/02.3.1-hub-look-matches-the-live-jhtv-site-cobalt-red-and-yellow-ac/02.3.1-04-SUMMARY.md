# Phase 02.3.1 Plan 04: Lead figures from the run's own words Summary

**Every pair now has two deterministic SVG pictures drawn by the hub itself from the run data and the record text (a card with no text and a detail with titles, distinctive words, scores and shared words), carried in the payload with the words of plan 02, verified by an allow-list safety check and size caps, kept private in a locked hub, and pinned by six golden files and 22 tests.**

## What was built

- `src/mindrian_eureka/hub/figure_copy.py`: `FIGURE_COPY`, 29 strings under `G18.*` (card and detail titles and sentences, 7 labels, `(no title)`, 4 legend strings). ASCII, no dash, slots from the deck's `SLOT_NAMES` only. They travel in the payload, not in the deck (`test_deck_is_untouched`).
- `src/mindrian_eureka/hub/figures.py` (standard library plus `.contracts`, `.deck.fill`, `.figure_copy`): `card_figure(lead)`, `detail_figure(lead)`, `build_figures(payload)`, `figure_problems(svg)`, `legend_texts()`, `label_texts()`, `scale_for`, `bar_width`, `wrap`, `fold_title`, `seed`, the layout constants, `FIGURE_TAGS` (svg, title, desc, rect, line, circle, text), `FIGURE_ATTRS` (21), `CLASS_TABLE` (24 classes), the 8 palette values. Every shape carries its class and a light-palette `fill` or `stroke` fallback; every text carries `font-size` and `fill`. The card: paper, frame, the rule at x 1100 + 20 * (byte 0 modulo 3), 7 fan lines (decoration), two markers with ink corners, the dashed link (28 on, 32 off), 3 ticks, the meaning bar and the wording bar on the engine scale (`round(760 * t)`, at least 8), one ink square for each shared word (at most 8), a cobalt block for agreement, a yellow block with an ink stroke for a Potential eureka. The detail: two panels with an ink strip, labels, the title wrapped to 22 characters and 4 lines, 5 word chips each, the middle column (MEANING and WORDING with tracks, bars and 2-decimal numerals), the dashed link at y 400, the ink band with up to 6 shared-word chips or the two-line sentence, and the same blocks as thin bars at the top right.
- `contracts.py`: `RunPair.confirmed` (default None), `PayloadPair.reading_kind` and `confirmed`, `READING_KINDS`, `PayloadTerms`, `PayloadFigureSet`, `PayloadLegend`, `PayloadLabels`, `PayloadFigures`, `Payload.terms` and `Payload.figures` (both `NotRequired`).
- `reader.py`: `scores.second_signal.confirmed` of a wording-engine pair is kept when it is a boolean; else None; the unusual-pairs engine always None.
- `model.py`: `reading_kind` on every pair from the three fixed reading sentences; `confirmed` only when the run holds a boolean; after `pattern`, `payload["terms"] = build_terms(run.records, triples of all pairs)` and `payload["figures"] = build_figures(payload)`.
- `verify.py`: `terms` (ids of the payload, 5 or 6 words at most, lower-case letters and hyphens of 4 or more), `figures` (key set equals the pair keys, each card and detail a string under its cap with `figure_problems(...) == []`, legend and label strings through `_check_copy`), `reading_kind` in the three kinds, `confirmed` a boolean when present. Every problem names the figure key. A payload without `terms` or `figures` still verifies.
- `docs/brand/hub-figure-rules.md` (the encoding table with the built coordinates, the class vocabulary, the scale, the colour roles, the yellow decision, what a picture never shows, text and safety, sizes, the target's own picture, book citations, the proposed segment "lead figure", the golden rewrite procedure). `docs/brand/hub-model-rules.md` gained "Distinctive words" and "Figures" with the measured cost.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 3 - Plan-internal inconsistency] Chip width and panel padding**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (layout against the plan's width model)
- **Issue:** The plan's chip width `17 * letters + 30` with text at chip x + 15 leaves a chip text outside its chip by the plan's own width model (0.6 times the size, 10 percent margin) for words above 10 letters, and a 22-character title line at x + 24 ends outside a 520-unit panel by 9 units.
- **Fix:** chip width `18 * letters + 30` and a panel padding of 14. Recorded in hub-figure-rules.md.
- **Files modified:** figures.py
- **Commit:** a06df4d

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] The unusual-pairs engine read a second signal**
- **Found during:** Task 3
- **Issue:** The first reader change read `second_signal` of any engine; the plan says the unusual-pairs engine gives None.
- **Fix:** the flag is read for the wording engine only.
- **Files modified:** reader.py
- **Commit:** 86e23d2

**3. [Rule 1 - Bug] The band drew more than 6 chips**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Fix:** the band caps its chips at 6 (`BAND_CHIPS_MAX`).
- **Commit:** a06df4d

**4. [Rule 3 - Measurement] Size caps**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** The plan's start values 2048 and 4096 were the prototype's sizes; the prototype carried no fallback attributes. The first measurement gave 2526 and 5612 bytes.
- **Fix:** caps set by the plan's own rule (largest measured value rounded up to 1024): 4096 and 7168. The card hard ceiling 5054 holds with a margin of 1931 bytes.
- **Commit:** a06df4d, 42ffd18

### Decisions inside the plan's rules

- Titles are escaped with `html.escape(text, quote=True)` (the plan named `quote=False`), so a raw `onerror="` pattern can never appear in a figure string even when a title holds one. The rendered text is the same.
- Golden choice for the two detail cases: the first pair with 1 or more shared words and the first pair with no shared word are taken among the pairs other than the both-engines pair, so the six goldens are six different pictures (the rank 1 unusual pair shares no word, so the plan's literal rule would have stored the same detail twice).
- The engine name in a figure title is the payload short name with its first letter in lower case ("unusual-pairs engine", "wording engine"), the same form as the G17 band.
- A chip word longer than 27 characters is cut to 24 characters and three dots; a chip that would fall below its panel or band is not drawn; the description lists every word.
