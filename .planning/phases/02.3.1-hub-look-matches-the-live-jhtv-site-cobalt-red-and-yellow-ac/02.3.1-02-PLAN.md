## The rule (the contract of this plan)

1. Text of a record: its title, one space, its text.
2. Tokens: lower-case the text. Match the regular expression `[a-z][a-z-]{3,}` (a letter, then 3 or more letters or hyphens). Strip hyphens from both ends. Fold the plural (below). Drop a token that is in `STOP_WORDS` or has 3 letters or fewer.
3. Fold, in this order, for a token of the length shown: ends with `ies` and length above 5: replace `ies` by `y`. Else ends with `ses`, `xes`, `zes`, `ches` or `shes` and length above 5: drop `es`. Else ends with `s`, not with `ss`, `us` or `is`, and length above 4: drop `s`. (Fixes of the prototype: it turned "calculus" into "calculu" and "classes" into "classe"; the plan keeps the words whole.)
4. Weight: for record r and word w, `score = tf(w, r) * log((N + 1) / (df(w) + 0.5))`. `tf` is the count of w in the tokens of r. `df` is the number of records whose tokens hold w. `N` is the number of records of the run. The logarithm is the natural one.
5. Order: sort the words of r by `(-round(score, 9), word)`. The first 5 are the distinctive words of r. The first 30 are the depth list of r.
6. Shared words of a pair of records a and b: the words that are in the depth list of both. Sort by `(max(rank in a, rank in b), word)` where rank is the position in the depth list (0 is first). Keep at most 6.
7. Stop list (fixed, 180 words or fewer, lower case): a about above after again all also an and any are as at be because been before being between both but by can could did do does doing down during each few for from further had has have having he her here hers him his how i if in into is it its just may more most much must no nor not now of off on once only or other our out over own same she should so some such than that the their them then there these they this those through to too under until up use used uses using very was we were what when where which while who whom why will with would you your paper papers show shows shown present presents propose proposed approach method methods result results based new however thus one two three first second via within without cannot study studies work works given general case cases number set sets problem problems model models.
8. Limits (written in the rules file): English letters only; a record in another script has no word; folding is light, so "analyses" and "analysis" stay different; a word that is rare in this collection is distinctive here, not in general; two long records with one shared rare word can look alike without being alike.

Where the numbers come from: 5, 30 and 6 are the owner's parameters (brief of 2026-11-08). 0.5 and 1 in the idf are the owner's formula. 9 decimals for the rounding is the planner's choice (a float has about 15 to 17 significant digits, so 9 is far above any platform difference and far below any real score gap); it is recorded in the rules file.
