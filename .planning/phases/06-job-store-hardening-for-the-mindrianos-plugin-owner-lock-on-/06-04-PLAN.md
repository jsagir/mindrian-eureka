<objective>
Make the cancel flag a persisted flag that the worker checks between steps, and make the result of a cancelled job say where and why it stopped.

Purpose: the plugin treats cancel as cooperative and polls for the terminal state (`eureka-capability-jobs.cjs:222-239`, `:250-263`). Today the persisted flag is written (`jobs.py:192`) but never read, and `_execute` wipes it at the first step (`jobs.py:373`); the worker sees only a process-local event (`jobs.py:380,387,397`), and a cancelled result names no stage (`jobs.py:199-205`). Criterion 4 of the ROADMAP.

Output: `tests/test_job_cancel_flag.py`, the flag logic in `jobs.py`, one RED commit and one GREEN commit.
</objective>
