<objective>
Bind the life of the engine process to its jobs. When the host closes the stdio transport, every job of that process ends `cancelled` with `cancel_reason host_closed` within one step and the process exits. A test with a sleeping fake engine inside the real `eureka-mcp` proves it.

Purpose: the plugin assumes a dead host never leaves a running engine (`eureka-capability-jobs.cjs:99`, "cannot resume after that process restarts"). Fact F6 proves the opposite today: with a job running, `eureka-mcp` does not exit after stdin closes because `main()` (`mcp_server.py:648-650`) shuts nothing down and the pool thread (`jobs.py:348`) is joined at interpreter exit. Criterion 5 of the ROADMAP, mapped per D-05 (see contract point C1).

Output: the fixture, the test file, `JobManager.close`, the `finally` in `main()`, one RED commit and one GREEN commit.
</objective>
