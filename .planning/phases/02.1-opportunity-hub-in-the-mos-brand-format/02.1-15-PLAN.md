<objective>
Build the Connection graph view without the 3D stage: the guide, the legend, Find, the static diagram, the list of pairs, the bridge tour, the shared fallback, the layout, and the hook contract that plan 18 fills with the 3D map.

Purpose: HUB-01 (view two of three, offline, from a file) and HUB-02 (the graph guide, colour meaning and Theo-face states of the book are implemented as ruled, and the one deviation from the guide is on record).

Locked decisions this plan implements:
- D-05: earn the graph first (at least 15 records in visible pairs); below that a static diagram.
- D-09: record = sphere, pair = line; the two pair kinds are named in words in the legend, tip and panel, never by colour alone.
- D-10: cobalt = a route under inspection; yellow = possibility, as a chip with ink text on yellow, never yellow text on paper; candidate pairs are unverified: dashed line in the diagram and the printed word "Proposed"; a blue route is attention, not proof.
- D-11: neighborhoods are numbered and tinted, always also named in words.
- D-12: if WebGL or the library is not available, the page shows the list of pairs (this plan owns the shared fallback and the list; plan 18 owns the loader).
- D-13: spheres and curves are fine in the graph; hard edges stay on panels and gates.
- D-14 (OWNER-WORD default): potential eurekas and the tour "Show me the potential eurekas". If the owner dropped bridges in plan 01, remove the chip, the tour and the strings Gr09 and P07, and say so in the SUMMARY.
- D-21: no ambient motion in the view; no particles on any line. This is a deviation from the book graph guide (GUIDE lines 17 to 27 draw bridge links thick yellow with red particles). It is recorded in code comments and in docs/brand/hub-model-rules.md, and in docs/brand/hub-design-decisions.md by plan 01.
- D-22: 13 px floor, 44 px targets, the non-pointer route through Find and the lists.

UI-SPEC section 6.2 (including 6.2a and 6.2b), section 7 and section 11.1 and 11.2 are the contract. Deck IDs: Gr01 to Gr10, Gr12, Gr16 to Gr19, B05, B06, P05. The IDs Gr11, Gr13, Gr14 and Gr15 belong to plan 18.

Split with plan 18. This plan writes hub-graph.js and hub-graph.css first. Plan 18 (wave 7) edits the same two files and writes the 3D stage on the hook contract of Task 1 step 2. A built hub between the two plans stays usable: the earned case ends in the shared fallback.

Parallel note: plans 11 to 15 run in the same wave and write sibling files in the same authored folder. The static rule tests (tests/test_hub_css.py and tests/test_hub_js_rules.py) scan every authored file. If one fails on a file this plan does not own, a sibling plan may still be writing it: wait, run the test again, and do not edit that file.
