# Mindrian job: UX and UI: pages, screens, forms and flows

Declare the work, read only what it routes to, tell the story with mindrian-storyteller, and test it before it ships.

## Declare

Intake surfaces: `website`, `app-ui`, `form`, `faq`. Run `npx --no-install mos-brand doctor`, then `npx --no-install mos-brand context`.

- `identity/theo/states` - The face state contract (working-baseline)

- `experience/faq` - FAQs and help: answer the decision behind the question (working-baseline)

Walk each route: ROOM.md for its one job, INDEX.md to go smaller, CONTEXT.md for inputs, process, outputs and the human check, STATE.md for status, then only the artifacts the job needs. The human check is the gate for work in that segment.

## Depth of guidance

Thin: `experience/layout` holds 1 artifact; `experience/narrative` holds 1 artifact; `experience/accessibility` holds 1 artifact; `identity/logo` holds 1 artifact; `identity/theo/states` holds 1 artifact; `teams/crm` holds 2 artifacts; `experience/faq` holds 2 artifacts. Apply the voice, the positioning and the stops below, and name every gap you meet in the missing-material list.

## Stops

- Never use an em dash. Plain English, short sentences.
- Never invent a number, quote, customer or result. Missing proof goes in a missing-material list.
- Highlighting never upgrades evidence status. Label facts, inferences and proposals.
- A breakthrough is an ambition, never a promise. MindrianOS is not autonomous, and the person decides.
- A person approves before anything publishes.
