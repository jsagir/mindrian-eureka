# Audiences: one core, many tellings

## The core that never changes

- Mindrian helps you navigate a growing project, explore possibilities and choose what to test next.
- It is a quest through uncertainty. The target can move; learning and the world change it.
- Three questions stay connected: a problem worth solving, a solution that works, a business case that holds.
- MindrianOS leads. The data room is the developing project. Theo supports the inquiry.
- The person keeps the judgment and decides what holds.
- A breakthrough is an ambition, never a promised output. MindrianOS is not autonomous, and Theo is not omniscient.

## What may change

Vocabulary, analogy, order, depth, length and the kind of proof. Nothing else.

## What may not change

The claims above. No invented numbers, results, customers or testimonials. No promise of a breakthrough.

## The ladder

Each row is a starting point, not a script. The sample lines are explanations, not facts about results.

| Listener | Open with | Use | Avoid | Proof they accept | Ask |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A child | Something they want to build that nobody has built | A clubhouse, a map, a helper that keeps ideas safe | Business words, abstract nouns, long sentences | Seeing it work on their own small idea | Try one idea together |
| A parent or grandparent | Someone they care about figuring out something new | A workshop table with everything laid out | Jargon, acronyms, hype | What it does for that person | Ask the person to show them |
| A curious adult | A project that grew bigger than one head can hold | A map that gets redrawn as you learn | Methods by name | One concrete example | Try it on one real question |
| A student | A question they cannot yet answer | The scientific habit of testing a guess | Promising answers | A worked example and its limits | Run one small test |
| An innovator or founder | The problem, the discovery or the opportunity they already hold | The three connected questions | Treating them as steps | Their own project laid out | Start from any one of the three |
| A researcher | What is known, assumed and disputed | Claims with a visible status | Hype, unlabelled inference | Sources, limits, what is not claimed | Check one claim against its source |
| A technical person | What it does and what it refuses to do | The boundary: no autonomy, a person decides | Magic words | Facts to verify with the product owner | Inspect the behavior directly |
| An investor or funder | The ambition and its honest limits | The uncertainty and how it is managed | Guaranteed outcomes | Labelled evidence, missing material stated | Name the evidence they would need |
| A skeptic | Their strongest objection | Their own words back to them | Defensiveness | The weakest point, shown first | Test the claim they doubt most |

## Sample lines

For each, check the core still holds.

- Child: Imagine you want to build something nobody has built yet. You are not sure what will work, and the rules might change. Mindrian is a helper that keeps your ideas, questions and tests in one place, and Theo asks what to try next. You still choose.
- Parent or grandparent: Mindrian is a workspace for someone figuring out something new and uncertain. It keeps their notes, questions and evidence connected, and a thinking partner called Theo helps them decide what to try next. They stay in charge.
- Innovator: You have a problem, a possible solution or an opportunity. Mindrian helps you keep the problem worth solving, the solution that works and the business case that holds connected as each one changes, and pick the next test.
- Researcher: Mindrian lays out a developing project as connected evidence, assumptions and questions, with Theo helping you examine relationships and gaps. Proposals stay proposals until you decide what holds.
- Technical: Product behavior is not in the brand book. State only the positioning above, and mark every technical claim as [verify with the product owner].
