## Baselines

1. Existing MindrianOS local retrieval and `/mos:find-connections` behavior on the same authorized graph snapshot.
2. Degree/common-neighbor baseline for missing links.
3. Local-only retrieval versus structural/semantic cross-domain candidate generation.
4. Current Theo workflow for the same wicked challenge, with blinded assessment.

## Experiments

- **Nested retrieval:** compare current retrieval, multi-resolution projections, and navigation; measure recall, precision, source completeness, latency, and tokens.
- **Structural growth:** repeatedly add nested/shared knowledge; measure stable references, duplicate detection, cycles, and meaning preservation.
- **Contradiction preservation:** provide conflicting claims and verify both persist with provenance and explicit conflict status.
- **Cross-domain discovery:** distribute relevant evidence across distant contexts; compare local-only, graph traversal, and proposed algorithms with negative controls.
- **Human gate:** verify proposals remain pending until review, rejection leaves accepted state untouched, approval has decision lineage, and reversal is possible.
- **Authorization:** attempt direct and indirect access to restricted room content; check contents and metadata leakage.
- **Scale:** test deep, wide, shared, cyclic, contradictory, repeated-ingestion, and partial-source snapshots.

## Metrics and failure criteria

- Candidate recall at bounded review volume; precision/usefulness on blinded judgments.
- Evidence traceability and completeness; false causal/novelty claims.
- Calibration kept separate for model confidence, evidence strength, novelty, and validity.
- Runtime, memory, payload size, and cost by method.
- Any unauthorized disclosure, silent promotion, evidence fabrication, or unrecoverable mutation is a hard failure.
- Do not declare success based only on more graph edges, candidates, or agreement between correlated algorithms.
