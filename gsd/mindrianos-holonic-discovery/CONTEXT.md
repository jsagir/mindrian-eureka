# Context for future implementation

## Product and method

MindrianOS is intended to turn fragmented research and experience into cumulative, traceable understanding and discovery. The intended tagline for this engine family is **“Extracting opportunities hidden in plain sight.”** Wicked and uncertain problems are a central discovery terrain. Follow the supplied distinction between undefined, ill-defined, and well-defined problems: future-state and opportunity exploration should not be forced into premature solution design.

## Terminology

- **Holon:** proposed boundary-bearing unit, both whole and part; exact host mapping is unknown.
- **Boundary:** semantic scope is distinct from security authorization.
- **Portal:** proposed explicit navigation relation; not every edge is a portal.
- **Projection:** task-specific context view with evidence references, version and explicit omission policy.
- **Focus Session:** proposed navigation/evidence history; not proof of resulting claims.
- **Discovery Candidate:** a pending relationship/hypothesis, not accepted knowledge.
- **Uncertainty:** opportunity condition to explore, not a defect the engine must erase.

## Relationships to existing MindrianOS methods

- **ICM:** reported as knowledge organization and run/audit structure; verify current implementation before mapping.
- **DataRoom:** reported as persistent problem-oriented workspace; verify database, store, and registry behavior.
- **Theo:** host reasoning and methodology service; Eureka must not claim Theo reviewed a case unless it received that case through a real interface.
- **PWS:** Problem Worth Solving methods guide the search from uncertain opportunity spaces toward well-defined testable problems.
- **Genesis:** MindrianOS retains its separate research-orchestration workflow. Eureka exposes LATEST's local planning modules as an experimental CLI/MCP job over a host-authorized graph artifact projection; external research, ICM persistence, and graph writes remain host-owned.
- **OM-HMM:** experimental decision-sequence comparison; distinct from opportunity ranking.
- **Eureka graph-relations:** source algorithms are candidate generators and structural diagnostics; historical predictive backtest failed.

## Constraints to preserve

- Reuse host governance and graph APIs; do not create parallel room access or portfolio state.
- Keep accepted knowledge separate from hypotheses and preserve conflicting claims.
- Keep endpoint identity, provenance, time, omissions, and review decisions traceable.
- Do not infer permission from room/scope IDs or graph links.
- Do not elevate graph score, semantic similarity, or algorithm agreement into truth or opportunity quality.
- Preserve source hashes, license status, test evidence, and maturity in the catalog and source ledger.
