## Intent

Rebuild the MindrianOS explainer around the user's supplied story and the ladder of abstraction. Move deliberately between concrete source material, abstract views of relationships and portfolio behavior, and concrete evidence/tests. Theo's geometric face is the lead character and the guide between levels: its eyes focus attention, its bar changes posture with the inquiry, and its lines become the system map. The visual language should feel like line art in motion, using the user's Swiss Signal reference as the design system.

## Assets

- `assets/theo-face-rest.svg` — canonical Theo eye-and-bar shape, adapted into the six reasoning poses.
- `assets/fonts/figtree.woff2` — display type.
- `assets/fonts/dm-sans-latin.woff2` — reading type.
- `assets/fonts/jetbrains-mono.ttf` — evidence labels and chapter markers.

## Customizations

- Make the face itself tell the story: eyes notice and redirect; the bar questions, searches, recognizes, and becomes an outline at the human handoff.
- Animate line-art connections, evidence states, and opportunity branches. Keep the change of direction visible without erasing earlier reasoning.
- Use the existing Swiss Signal colors and editorial rules: paper `#F7F5EE`, ink `#111111`, evidence blue `#1F3FBF`, signal red `#D6281F`, tension yellow `#F2C200`, muted ink `#4B4943`.
- Keep narration and on-screen wording understandable without motion. Make evidence, assumptions, proposals, and human decisions distinct.

## Notes

- The existing 57-second narration belongs to the previous technology example. The new story needs a newly timed narration track; do not reuse that audio as if it matches this script.
- The requested face is the hero throughout. Other marks and diagrams grow from its eyes and bar rather than replacing it with generic product UI.
- The target duration is a planning estimate. Final scene timing should follow the approved narration and preserve readable holds.
- Do not imply every opportunity is validated or that MindrianOS chooses what to pursue. The person retains judgment and direction.
