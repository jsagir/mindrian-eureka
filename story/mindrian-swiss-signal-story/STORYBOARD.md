## Story spine

One blue reasoning line moves through every level: E-17 source underline → Theo's bar → opportunity-system connector → evidence pattern → source drill-down → revised model → shared portfolio dependency → test protocol → result ripple. Scale changes must preserve provenance.

## Brand and evidence grammar

Follow `frame.md`. Paper, ink, blue evidence, red assumption/conflict, yellow uncertainty/decision, and muted dotted proposal/history are semantic—not decorative. All project details are labelled ILLUSTRATIVE.

## Bans

No generic dashboard UI, rounded cards, gradients, shadows, stock imagery, detached mascot, unsupported claims, or erased history. Do not duplicate narration in on-screen paragraphs. Keep the lower 184px clear.

## Held frame

Frame 8 is the deliberate hold: the uncertainty, protocol, observation, threshold, and disconfirming result must all remain legible before the result returns to the wider system.
