# Story direction

This video tells people exploring MindrianOS that Mindrian is being built to turn scattered research into an opportunity portfolio they can revisit, deepen, and act on.

Theo stays outside the problem space as its thinker. The actual Theo face system moves through rest, search, finding, listening, challenge, decision, and handover poses. Inside the space, paper planes and square tiles carry the work: source material, evidence, assumptions, contradictions, and the human decision gate. The storyboard is an illustrative product vision; no fabricated opportunity, score, customer, or live-room data is shown.
