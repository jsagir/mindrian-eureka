# Higgs-field visual direction

This is a new visual version of the MindrianOS explainer. The previous cut and narration remain in `../mindrian-opportunity-story/`. The Higgs field is used only as a visual analogy for shared context: a quiet, structured plane makes relationships easier to see as material, questions, evidence, and decisions are linked. It does not imply that MindrianOS creates answers or that opportunities are physically generated.

The visual language follows the Mindrian website and Theo-face system: warm paper, hard ink rules, flat square tiles, blue evidence, red assumptions or conflicts, yellow open questions, and the canonical Theo face. Each frame changes the field state while Theo moves through the inquiry from rest to search, finding, listening, exploration, challenge, decision, and handover.

The existing ElevenLabs narration is reused unchanged for continuity and exact timing. On-screen copy marks the metaphor and its limits. All sample artifacts are illustrative, not live room data.
