## Intent

Recreate the MindrianOS opportunity-discovery explainer with the Higgs field as a restrained visual metaphor. Begin with scattered research artifacts in a quiet shared field. As the user frames a question, show relevant materials connecting to evidence, candidates, counterchecks, and human decisions. Finish with a portfolio that can inform later questions.

The metaphor is limited: MindrianOS is a research workspace, not a physical field, and it does not create or prove breakthroughs. Evidence, method selection, and human review determine whether a candidate deserves further attention.
